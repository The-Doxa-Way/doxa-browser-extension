Forgetting is one of the oldest human struggles — Israel built stone memorials at the Jordan River precisely because they knew they would forget what God had done there. The discipline of remembering has to be practiced and protected. Joshua 4:6-7 puts it plainly: those stones were to be a sign, so that when your children ask, you can tell them what God did. Write down one thing God has done for you — a specific moment — so that when the fog of forgetfulness rolls in, you have something concrete to return to. — Doxa · doxa.app
